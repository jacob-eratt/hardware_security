\section{Conclusion}
Our initial results demonstrate that a GRU can successfully
learn tree-PLRU replacement behavior from memory-access
histories, achieving nearly perfect prediction accuracy
across multiple cache associativities. This validates our
simulation and machine-learning pipeline as a foundation
for characterizing cache replacement policies. On histories
with misses, accuracy decreased as misses increased for both
DRRIP and PLRU, which identifies tracking blocks across
misses as the main challenge.

Moving forward, we plan to improve accuracy on histories
with misses, evaluate the model on longer access histories, compare
predictions against other replacement policies, and collect
eviction measurements from the L2 cache of an Intel Celeron
N3450. Ultimately,
we aim to determine whether neural sequence models can
reliably identify undocumented cache replacement behavior
and support hardware security research.
